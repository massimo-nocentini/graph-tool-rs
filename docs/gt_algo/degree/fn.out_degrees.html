Fill a vertex map with out-degrees.

The map is grown to g.vertex_bound() – the unfiltered allocation bound, never num_vertices – and then the whole run is cleared before the surviving vertices are written. Both halves matter:

  • sizing from the bound is the half copy_property gets wrong (graph_copy.cc:66-73 reserves the filtered count and writes at unfiltered indices);
  • clearing is what makes the answer a function of g alone. A map reused across two calls, or one filled by an earlier kernel, would otherwise report a stale degree at every vertex this view filters out – and get_unchecked(size = 0) (dispatch.hh:171-177) hands graph-tool exactly such a map.

Panics if out belongs to a different graph than g; a map reaching a kernel has been sized for it, and the GraphId comparison happens once here rather than per access.
