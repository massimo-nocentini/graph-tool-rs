Sum of a weight map over every edge incident to v.

total_degreeS (graph_selectors.hh:213-246) is in_degreeS + out_degreeS on a directed graph and out_degreeS alone on an undirected one. Walking all_edges is both at once, because the adjacency block is the two halves concatenated and an undirected view’s all_edges is that same run (D2). A self-loop occupies one slot in each half and is therefore counted twice, which is what degree(v, g) (graph_adjacency.hh:1069-1073) says.
